# GreenLeaf Systems — first call

*Note, 21 July 2026. Written straight after the call.*

Spoke to Nadia Hoque, COO. Thirty minutes, introduced by Kestrel.

GreenLeaf run soil and yield telemetry for about four hundred farms. Their
problem is the classic one: three systems, none of them authoritative, and a
reporting layer that someone in finance maintains by hand every Monday.

Nadia was direct. Two things she said that matter:

1. "I don't want a platform. I want to stop the Monday thing."
2. They have been burned before — a previous vendor took eight months on
   something scoped at three.

Budget was not discussed. She raised it and then moved past it, which I read as
"there is money but I'm not showing it yet."

Kickoff booked for Wednesday 29 July at 14:30. Priya is leading; I'll be on the
call. She's asked us to come with a view rather than a questionnaire.

No commitments made on either side.
