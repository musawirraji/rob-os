# Aisle3 — Services and Engagement Model

*Sales deck, v4. Last revised 12 June 2026.*

---

## Slide 1 — What we do

Aisle3 builds and runs operational data platforms for mid-market companies that
have outgrown spreadsheets but cannot justify a platform team.

Founded 2023. Eleven people. London.

---

## Slide 2 — The three tiers

Every engagement is priced across three tiers. Clients can buy one, two, or all
three, but the tiers are always priced separately so the cost of each is visible.

**Tier 1 — Discovery and data audit**
Three weeks. Fixed fee. £18,000.
We map every system that holds operational data, document what is authoritative
where, and produce a migration plan.

**Tier 2 — Build and integration**
Six to twelve weeks depending on system count. £52,000–£96,000.
Pipelines, warehouse, and the integrations into whatever the client already runs.

**Tier 3 — Ongoing support**
Rolling monthly or annual up front. £3,200/month, or £34,000 annually.
Monitoring, incident response, and up to sixteen hours of change work a month.

---

## Slide 3 — What a typical engagement costs

A mid-market client buying all three tiers lands between £82,000 and £130,000
for the first year.

Data migration from a legacy system, where required, is quoted separately and
typically runs £8,000–£15,000.

---

## Slide 4 — How we work

- One senior engineer and one delivery lead on every engagement, minimum.
- Weekly written progress note. No status meetings unless asked for.
- Everything we build is documented to the point where the client's own team
  could take it over. We have had clients do exactly that, and we consider it a
  success.

---

## Slide 5 — Selected work

- **Northwind Retail** — replaced fourteen spreadsheets and two Access databases
  with a single warehouse. Nine weeks. Now run entirely in-house.
- **Basepoint** — operational reporting layer across four regional entities.
- **Kestrel Logistics** — real-time fleet telemetry pipeline, 2.1M events/day.
